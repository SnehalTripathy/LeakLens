# LeakLens

**Find the leaks. Understand the losses. Protect your profits.**

LeakLens is a financial intelligence workspace that helps businesses understand where their money goes, identify unusual spending patterns, and make more informed financial decisions.

[**Visit the LeakLens website →**](https://leaklens-frontend-xhdn.onrender.com/)

## Overview

Financial data often contains patterns that are difficult to spot through spreadsheets and basic reports. LeakLens brings transaction analysis, anomaly detection, forecasting, budgeting, and actionable insights together in one interactive dashboard.

It is designed to turn raw financial activity into a clearer picture of business performance and potential areas of concern.

## Key Features

- **Financial overview:** Monitor revenue, expenses, balances, cash movement, and category-level performance.
- **Transaction analysis:** Import and review transaction records, with support for CSV, XLS, and XLSX files.
- **Unusual-spend detection:** Identify potential duplicate payments, unexpected vendor increases, recurring charges, and other patterns that may need attention.
- **Financial trends and forecasts:** Explore historical performance, projected revenue and expenses, and forecast accuracy.
- **Budget monitoring:** Set category budgets and compare actual spending with budget limits and projections.
- **Vendor and subscription insights:** Understand vendor concentration, recurring payments, and subscription costs.
- **What-if simulation:** Explore how potential changes in revenue, expenses, and one-time costs could affect financial outcomes.
- **Recommendations:** Surface practical areas to investigate and opportunities to improve cost control.
- **Financial assistant:** Ask questions about the current workspace and get responses grounded in its financial data.
- **Reports and exports:** Review alerts and data quality, and export transaction data and financial summaries.

## How It Works

LeakLens brings together transaction rules, statistical analysis, and forecasting methods to highlight financial activity that may deserve a closer look.

The workflow is designed to help users:

1. Bring transaction data into a single workspace.
2. Review financial performance across categories, vendors, and reporting periods.
3. Identify unusual activity and investigate the transactions behind each finding.
4. Explore trends, forecasts, budgets, and possible cost-control actions.
5. Use reports and insights to support better-informed decisions.

LeakLens findings are decision-support signals, not proof of fraud or wrongdoing. Forecasts and estimated exposures are indicative and depend on the quality and coverage of the available data.

## Technology

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** SQLite
- **Analytics:** Financial rules, statistical anomaly detection, and forecasting
- **Reporting:** Interactive visualisations, CSV exports, and PDF summaries
