# LeakLens

**Find the leaks. Understand the losses. Protect your profits.**

LeakLens is a financial intelligence platform that helps businesses make sense of their financial activity. It brings transaction analysis, anomaly detection, forecasting, and cost-control insights into one interactive workspace.

## Features

- **Financial dashboard** — Track revenue, expenses, balances, cash movement, and category performance.
- **Transaction analysis** — Import and review financial records in CSV, XLS, and XLSX formats.
- **Anomaly detection** — Flag potential duplicate payments, unusual vendor increases, recurring charges, and unexpected spending patterns.
- **Forecasting and trends** — Explore historical performance, projected revenue and expenses, and forecast accuracy.
- **Budget management** — Compare category-level budgets with actual and projected spending.
- **Vendor and subscription insights** — Examine vendor concentration, recurring payments, and subscription costs.
- **What-if analysis** — Model potential changes in revenue, expenses, and one-time costs.
- **Actionable recommendations** — Identify areas that may warrant investigation and opportunities for better cost control.
- **Financial assistant** — Ask questions about the financial data in the current workspace.
- **Reports and exports** — Review alerts and data quality, and export transaction data and financial summaries.

## How It Works

LeakLens combines transaction rules, statistical analysis, and forecasting methods to turn raw financial records into useful business insights. Users can explore performance, investigate unusual activity, understand financial trends, and evaluate potential cost-control decisions in one place.

Anomaly flags are decision-support signals, not proof of fraud or wrongdoing. Forecasts and estimated exposures are indicative and depend on the quality and coverage of the available data.

## Project Objective

LeakLens explores how data analytics can make financial oversight more proactive and accessible. By bringing transaction visibility, anomaly signals, forecasting, and scenario analysis together, the project aims to help users move beyond simply tracking what has been spent and towards understanding where attention may be needed next.
